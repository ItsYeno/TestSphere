Gas test before hot work or confined space entry, and repeat the test every 2 hours while the work continues.

Document HSE-PRO-011, revision 4. Owner: HSE.

Limits

Work may start only when oxygen is between 19.5 and 23.5 percent, flammable gas is below 10 percent of the lower explosive limit, and hydrogen sulphide is below 5 ppm.

Who may test

Only an authorised gas tester with a calibrated, bump-tested detector may sign the test on the permit.
