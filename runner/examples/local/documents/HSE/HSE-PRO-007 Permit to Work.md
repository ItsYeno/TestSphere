No maintenance work on process equipment may start without a valid permit to work signed by the area authority and the performing authority.

Document HSE-PRO-007, revision 8. Owner: HSE.

Permit types

Cold work, hot work, confined space entry and electrical work each have their own permit. Hot work and confined space entry also need a gas test (HSE-PRO-011).

Validity

A permit is valid for one shift. It must be displayed at the work site and returned to the control room when the work stops.
