To isolate a pump before maintenance work, open and rack out the motor breaker, close the suction and discharge valves, and apply a personal lock-out tag-out at every isolation point.

Document MNT-PRO-022, revision 5. Owner: Maintenance.

Steps

1. Obtain a permit to work (see HSE-PRO-007).
2. Stop the pump from the control room and confirm it has stopped.
3. Open the motor breaker and rack it out where the switchgear allows.
4. Close and lock the suction and discharge valves.
5. Depressurise and drain the casing to the closed drain.
6. Each person working on the pump applies their own lock and tag.
7. Try to start the pump from the local station to prove the isolation before touching it.

Removing the isolation

Locks are removed only by the person who applied them, after the permit to work is signed off.
