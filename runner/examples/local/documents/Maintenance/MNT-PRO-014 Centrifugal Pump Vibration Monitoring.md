Raise a vibration alarm on any centrifugal pump when overall velocity reaches 7 mm/s RMS, and trip the pump when it reaches 11 mm/s RMS.

Document MNT-PRO-014, revision 3. Owner: Rotating Equipment, Maintenance.

Scope

This procedure applies to centrifugal pumps driven by electric motors above 15 kW in process and utility service.

Measurement

Readings are taken at the drive-end and non-drive-end bearing housings in the horizontal, vertical and axial directions. Online monitoring flags a reading that stays above the alarm limit for more than 60 seconds.

Response to an alarm

1. Confirm the reading with a portable analyser within 4 hours.
2. Check for cavitation, misalignment, looseness and bearing wear.
3. Raise a work order and inform the area HSE officer.
4. If the reading keeps rising, plan a controlled shutdown before it reaches the trip limit.

Response to a trip

Do not restart the pump until the cause is found and the shift supervisor approves the restart. Isolate the pump following MNT-PRO-022 before any inspection.
